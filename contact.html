Common Questions

How can I ask about my order?
Contact CIRDRIO by email and include your order number, registered email address and the issue you are experiencing.
What if my product arrives damaged?
Contact CIRDRIO promptly and retain the product and packaging. Photos of the damage and packaging may be requested to help assess the issue.
Can I cancel my order?
Cancellation depends on the current fulfilment status of the order. Please contact CIRDRIO as early as possible.
Where can I find the return policy?
Our Returns & Refunds Policy explains the general return, refund and eligibility framework.
How long does a refund take?
Once a refund is approved and initiated, the time required for the funds to appear may depend on the relevant payment provider or financial institution.
