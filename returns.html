1. Returns & Refunds Overview

CIRDRIO aims to make the returns and refund process clear, reasonable and convenient while protecting product quality, customer safety and transaction integrity.

Return and refund eligibility depends on the product, order status, condition of the item, reason for the request and applicable law.

Important: Do not send a product back without first following the applicable CIRDRIO return process or receiving return instructions where required.

2. Return Eligibility

A return may be considered where the product is eligible under the applicable CIRDRIO return policy and the request is made within the applicable return period.

Where a return is accepted, the product will generally need to be returned in an appropriate condition, together with its original packaging and included components where applicable.

Additional requirements may apply depending on the product category and reason for the return.

3. Products That May Not Be Returnable

For hygiene, health, safety or product-integrity reasons, certain products may not be eligible for return once opened, used or otherwise handled beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect the product.

Examples may include products where returning an opened or used item would create a hygiene or safety concern.

Any applicable exclusion will be subject to mandatory consumer rights under applicable law.

4. Damaged Products

If your order arrives damaged, contact CIRDRIO as soon as reasonably possible.

For efficient assessment, you may be asked to provide photographs of the product, packaging, shipping label and visible damage.

Please retain the product and original packaging until CIRDRIO or the applicable delivery partner provides further instructions.

5. Incorrect or Missing Products

If you receive an incorrect product or an item appears to be missing from your order, contact CIRDRIO promptly with your order details.

We may verify the order, fulfilment record and shipment information before determining the appropriate resolution.

Where an error is confirmed, CIRDRIO may provide an eligible replacement, return, refund or another appropriate resolution.

6. How to Request a Return

01 · Contact

Contact CIRDRIO with your order information and reason for the request.

02 · Review

Our team may review the order and determine whether the request is eligible.

03 · Instructions

If approved, you will receive the applicable return or resolution instructions.

04 · Resolution

After the required verification, an eligible replacement or refund will be processed.

7. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be processed using the applicable payment method or refund mechanism.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider, bank or financial institution.

Refund amounts may be affected by the specific circumstances of the return and applicable policy, subject to mandatory consumer rights.

8. Return Shipping

Responsibility for return shipping may depend on the reason for the return and the applicable CIRDRIO policy.

Where CIRDRIO provides specific return instructions, customers should follow those instructions carefully.

Products should be packaged securely to reduce the risk of damage during return transit.

9. Return Review & Rejection

A return may be rejected where the product does not meet the applicable eligibility requirements, subject to applicable law.

Examples may include unauthorized returns, products that show significant use or damage caused after delivery, missing components, or requests made outside the applicable return period.

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer protection right that cannot legally be excluded.

10. Returns Support

For a return, refund, damaged-product or incorrect-order request, contact CIRDRIO with your order details and a clear description of the issue.
