How technology remembers.

This disclosure explains how CIRDRIO may use cookies, browser storage and similar technologies to operate, remember preferences and improve the shopping experience.

1. Overview

CIRDRIO may use cookies and similar technologies to provide, secure and improve its website and services.

These technologies can allow a website to remember information between visits, maintain functionality and understand how visitors interact with the website.

Important: The technologies actually used by CIRDRIO may change as the website, analytics systems and third-party services evolve.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small data files that a website may store in a user's browser. They can help websites recognise a browser or remember certain information.

Depending on how CIRDRIO operates its website, cookies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Maintaining website functionality.
  • Remembering certain preferences.
  • Supporting account or session functionality.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
  • Understanding website usage where analytics are enabled.

3. Browser & Local Storage

CIRDRIO may also use browser technologies such as local storage where appropriate for website functionality.

For example, shopping-cart information may be stored locally in your browser so that items can remain available while you navigate the website.

Browser-stored information can generally be removed through your browser settings, although doing so may affect certain website features.

4. Essential Technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for the website to function properly.

These may support functions such as account sessions, authentication, shopping-cart functionality, security and other core website operations.

Disabling essential technologies may prevent some parts of the website from working correctly.

5. Analytics

If analytics tools are enabled on the CIRDRIO website, they may collect information about website usage, such as pages viewed, general interaction patterns, device information or technical performance data.

Analytics are intended to help CIRDRIO understand website performance and improve the customer experience.

The specific analytics providers and technologies used may change over time.

6. Third-Party Services

CIRDRIO may use third-party technology providers to operate parts of its website or business infrastructure.

These providers may process certain technical information or use cookies and similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable contractual arrangements.

Examples of third-party infrastructure may include hosting, authentication, storage, payment, analytics or other service providers.

Where applicable, third-party providers' own privacy and cookie policies may also apply.

7. Your Choices

Most modern browsers allow users to control or delete cookies through browser settings.

You may also be able to control certain website storage and tracking technologies through your device or browser settings.

However, restricting essential technologies may affect the availability or functionality of some CIRDRIO features.

Browser controls: Cookie and storage controls vary between browsers and devices. For precise instructions, consult the settings or help information provided by your browser or device manufacturer.

8. Changes to This Disclosure

CIRDRIO may update this Cookie & Tracking Disclosure when website functionality, technology, legal requirements or business practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
