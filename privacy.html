This Privacy Policy explains how CIRDRIO collects, uses, stores, protects and manages information when you use our website, create an account, place an order or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

CIRDRIO collects information that you voluntarily provide when using our website and information that is necessary to operate our services.

Information you provide

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and billing address information
  • Account information
  • Order and purchase information
  • Information you provide when contacting support

Information generated through use

Depending on how you use the website, we may also process information associated with your account, orders, saved addresses, cart activity and other actions necessary to provide the requested service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your CIRDRIO account.
  • Process and fulfil orders.
  • Deliver products to the address you provide.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain and improve our website and services.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain business, security and operational records.
  • Communicate with you regarding your account or orders.

We aim to collect and use information only for legitimate business and service purposes relevant to your interaction with CIRDRIO.

3. Orders & Payments

When you place an order, we process information required to create and fulfil that order, including your contact details, delivery information, purchased products and order totals.

Payment information: CIRDRIO does not intend to store complete payment-card credentials on its own systems. When online payment services are introduced, payment processing will be handled through the applicable payment service provider under that provider's terms and privacy practices.

Where Cash on Delivery or another payment method is offered, relevant payment and order information may be recorded to manage the transaction.

4. Accounts & Profile Information

If you create a CIRDRIO account, information associated with your account may be stored so that we can authenticate you, maintain your profile and provide account-related services.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for activity performed through your account.

5. Saved Addresses

CIRDRIO may allow registered customers to save delivery addresses for faster checkout.

Saved addresses are associated with your account and are used to support checkout and delivery-related functionality.

You can review, update or remove saved address information through the available account functionality, subject to any information that must be retained for legitimate business, legal or transaction-record purposes.

6. When We Share Information

CIRDRIO does not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

We may share information where reasonably necessary with service providers and partners that help us operate the business, such as technology, hosting, payment, logistics, communication or customer-support providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, regulatory requirements, or where necessary to protect the rights, security or property of CIRDRIO, our customers or others.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore use appropriate care when sharing information online.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain accounts, fulfil and document transactions, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, prevent abuse and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

9. Your Choices & Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may also choose not to provide certain information. However, some information is necessary for account creation, checkout, delivery and other requested services.

To make a privacy-related request, contact CIRDRIO using the details provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

CIRDRIO's services are intended for users who are legally able to use the service and make purchases under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CIRDRIO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact CIRDRIO

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, contact us.
