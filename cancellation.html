Cancel with clarity.

A straightforward guide to when an order may be cancelled, what happens after cancellation, and how eligible refunds are handled.

1. Cancellation Overview

CIRDRIO understands that customers may occasionally need to cancel an order. Cancellation is handled according to the order's current fulfilment status and the applicable policy.

A cancellation request should be submitted as early as possible because orders may move into processing or shipment shortly after they are placed.

Important: Submitting a cancellation request does not automatically guarantee that the order can be cancelled.

2. Customer Cancellation Requests

Customers may contact CIRDRIO to request cancellation of an order before it has progressed beyond a stage where cancellation is reasonably possible.

When requesting cancellation, provide the order details and the information necessary for us to identify the order.

CIRDRIO will review the order status and communicate the applicable outcome.

3. Orders Already Being Processed

Once an order has entered fulfilment, packing or another operational stage, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If cancellation is not possible, the customer may instead have rights under the applicable returns and refund policy, subject to product eligibility and applicable law.

4. Orders Already Shipped

An order that has already been dispatched may generally not be cancelled through the normal pre-shipment cancellation process.

Where applicable, the customer may need to follow the relevant return process after delivery.

Customers should avoid refusing delivery solely as a substitute for following the applicable cancellation or return process unless CIRDRIO specifically instructs otherwise.

5. Cancellation by CIRDRIO

CIRDRIO may cancel an order in circumstances where fulfilment is not reasonably possible or where an order requires cancellation for legitimate operational, security or compliance reasons.

Examples may include significant inventory issues, inaccurate pricing or product information, suspected fraudulent activity, technical errors or circumstances beyond reasonable operational control.

Where an eligible prepaid order is cancelled by CIRDRIO, the applicable refund will be processed according to the payment method and circumstances of the order.

6. Refund After Cancellation

Where cancellation is successfully completed and a refund is due, CIRDRIO will initiate the applicable refund process.

The time taken for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider, bank or financial institution.

For Cash on Delivery orders that have been cancelled before payment, there is generally no payment amount to refund.

Any refund will remain subject to applicable law and the specific circumstances of the order.

7. If Cancellation Is No Longer Available

If an order cannot be cancelled because it has already progressed through fulfilment or shipment, CIRDRIO may direct the customer to the applicable Returns & Refunds Policy.

Return eligibility, product condition requirements and any applicable exceptions will be assessed under that policy.

8. Cancellation Support

For cancellation-related assistance, contact CIRDRIO as soon as possible and include your order information.
