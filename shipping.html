1. Shipping Overview

CIRDRIO aims to provide a clear, reliable and convenient delivery experience for every eligible order.

Shipping availability, delivery timelines and applicable charges may vary depending on the destination, product availability, logistics partner and other operational factors.

Important: A delivery estimate is an indication rather than a guaranteed delivery date unless CIRDRIO expressly states otherwise.

2. Order Processing

After an order is successfully placed, CIRDRIO may require time to verify, prepare and package the products before dispatch.

Stage 01

Order received — Your order is recorded in the CIRDRIO system.

Stage 02

Order confirmed — The order is reviewed and accepted for fulfilment.

Stage 03

Processing — Products are prepared and packaged.

Stage 04

Shipped — The package is handed to the applicable delivery partner.

Stage 05

Delivered — The package reaches the delivery destination.

3. Delivery

Orders are delivered to the address supplied during checkout or to an eligible saved address selected by the customer.

Customers should ensure that the recipient is available to receive the shipment where required by the delivery provider.

Delivery timelines can vary according to destination, logistics capacity, public holidays, weather, local conditions and other circumstances outside CIRDRIO's reasonable control.

4. Shipping Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, including the correct name, phone number, postal code and other required details.

CIRDRIO cannot guarantee successful delivery where an address is incorrect, incomplete or inaccessible.

If you identify an address error, contact CIRDRIO as soon as possible. We will attempt to assist where the order has not yet entered a stage where changes are no longer possible.

5. Delivery Delays

Occasional delays may occur because of circumstances beyond our reasonable control, including:

  • Severe weather or natural events
  • Public holidays and regional closures
  • Transportation or logistics disruptions
  • Operational issues with delivery partners
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery information
  • Unexpected demand or service interruptions

Where practical, CIRDRIO or its delivery partner may provide updated delivery information.

6. Failed or Missed Delivery

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful, the delivery partner may attempt redelivery or follow its applicable delivery procedure.

Repeated failed delivery attempts, refusal to accept a package or incorrect delivery information may result in the order being returned to the sender.

Any applicable refund, reshipment or additional delivery charges will be handled according to the relevant CIRDRIO policy and applicable law.

7. Order Tracking

Where tracking information is available, customers may receive delivery updates through the applicable order or communication system.

Tracking information is provided by the relevant logistics provider and may not update immediately at every stage of transit.

8. Damaged or Incorrect Packages

If your package arrives visibly damaged, contains the wrong product, or appears incomplete, contact CIRDRIO as soon as reasonably possible.

For faster assistance, keep the original packaging and provide relevant order information and photographs where requested.

Any resolution will be assessed under CIRDRIO's applicable Returns & Refund Policy and applicable consumer protection requirements.

9. Shipping Support

For questions about delivery, shipping or an order that has not arrived as expected, contact CIRDRIO.
